# Executive_sales_Dashboard Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard | An interactive Power BI dashboard providing insights into revenue, gross profit, orders, units sold, and profit margins. Features monthly sales trends, top-performing countries, regional analysis, and sales channel performance to support strategic business decision-making. Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance and profitability metrics, enabling executives to make data-driven business decisions. The dashboard tracks key KPIs such as Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Units Sold, Profit Margin, Total Orders, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
Key Features Executive-level KPI cards for quick performance evaluation Monthly Revenue vs Gross Profit trend analysis Top 5 Revenue-Generating Countries visualization Sales Channel performance analysis (Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, Retail Partners) Revenue distribution by sales channels Regional revenue contribution analysis across Europe Interactive slicers for dynamic data exploration Business Insights Monitor overall business profitability and sales growth Identify top-performing countries and regions Analyze revenue contribution across multiple sales channels Track monthly sales and profit trends for strategic planning Tools Used Data Visualization Interactive Dashboard Design Dashboard Objectives
The dashboard helps stakeholders understand revenue performance, profitability trends, customer channel effectiveness, and regional market contributions, supporting informed business decisions and strategic growth initiatives.
